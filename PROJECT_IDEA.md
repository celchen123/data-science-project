The issue I want to focus on is how heavy metals and other weather factors can worsen pollen allergies by affecting the pollen grain count. Specifically, I want to examine different factors like temperature, precipitation, wind and heavy metals like arsenic, cadmium, chromium, lead, manganese, nickel, and zinc. This issue is important as people all around the world suffer from pollen allergies, which can worsen respiratory issues such as asthma. In addition, heavy metal pollution is becoming more prominent as human society expands to nearly all areas of the Earth. As such, a model or identified relationship between pollen grain fluctuation and heavy metal air pollution is necessary.
